How it plays

Tap to slap

Hit the fly to score Slaps. Miss and you lose a heart. Lose all three and it's game over.

Don't stand still

An idle bar keeps draining. Go too long without a hit and the fly wins.

Super Slap

Swipe to launch a shockwave that rakes in Slaps. But if it hits nothing, your run is over.

The Climb

Earn Slaps by playing to climb 15 levels, from Fresh Meat to Slap Legend, unlocking new gear and rooms on the way.

Four power-ups. Twelve levels each.

Collect pickups during a run, then fire them off when the fly gets cocky. Upgrade them in the shop to make them stronger.

Freeze

Stops the fly cold, and every tap is worth more.

Stun

Knocks the fly to the ground, dizzy and wobbly.

Slow-Mo

Slows the whole world down for a cinematic swat.

Multi-Slap

Every hit lands several slaps at once.

Fair play

Your level comes only from playing. Purchases and ad rewards add Slaps you can spend, but they never raise your level.
