Support

Stuck, confused, or out-slapped? We can help.

Email us: rockyroy.studios@gmail.com
To help us sort it out faster, include your phone model, your Android version, the game version (shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings) and what happened. For a purchase problem, also include the order number from your store receipt email (Google Play order numbers start with GPA.; Apple order IDs are on your App Store receipt).

Common questions

My progress disappeared

Your progress is saved on your phone, not in an online account. It can be lost if the game is uninstalled, its storage is cleared, Reset progress is used, or you move to a new phone. Android's device backup may bring it back when you reinstall, depending on your backup settings.

We can't restore local progress. If you lost Slaps you paid for, email us with your Google Play order number.

I bought Slaps but didn't get them
  1. Make sure you're connected to the internet, then fully close and reopen the game.
  2. Open the in-game Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  3. If they still don't show up, email us with your Google Play order number.

Payments and refunds are handled by the store you bought from. Request a refund through Google Play's refund page or Apple's Report a Problem.

An ad didn't load, or I didn't get my reward

Rewarded ads need an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available right away. Try again in a minute. Rewards are only given after an ad finishes, so closing an ad early won't give the reward. Some rewards also have a daily limit or a short cooldown, shown on the reward card.

I picked the wrong age range

Open the in-game Settings and use Reset progress, then fully close and reopen the game. It will ask the age question again. Be careful: this permanently deletes your progress, including any Slaps you bought.

How do I replay the tutorial or turn off sound and vibration?

Open the in-game Settings. It has switches for music, sound effects and vibration, plus an option to replay the tutorials.

How do I delete my data?

To erase what's saved on your phone, use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or clear the app's storage in Android settings. To ask about data held by our analytics, crash-reporting or ad partners, email us. Our Privacy Policy explains what's collected and how.

I found a bug

Thank you! Email us what you were doing when it happened, plus your phone model and the game version. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
