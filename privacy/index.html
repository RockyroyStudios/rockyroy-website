Privacy Policy

Pesky Pest Fly Quest · Rockyroy Studios
Working draft for the planned Android release · Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version

  • The game has no player account. It saves game progress on your device and does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or files.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device, and we don't receive it.
  • The game asks for an age range before it initializes its ad and gameplay-analytics SDKs. Some platform or Firebase components can start with the app; we have not verified that absolutely no technical data is processed before the age choice.
  • Under 18: the game is configured to disable gameplay analytics and crash reporting and to request child-directed ads. Ad and mediation providers may still process technical information needed to handle an ad request.
  • 18 or older: the planned release will ask separately before optional crash reporting. Google consent choices will govern Google ads and, where implemented, Firebase Analytics. These flows are not all present in current test builds.
  • We do not sell personal information for money. Some advertising disclosures may count as “sharing” or targeted advertising under local laws; see Advertising and Your rights and choices.

Release note: This draft describes the planned initial Android release, not every internal test build. The current test build uses Unity LevelPlay and GameAnalytics; the planned Google Mobile Ads/UMP and Firebase Analytics integrations are not yet active. We will update this policy before enabling any new data flow. Do not treat it as a statement that the current test build already implements the described consent choices.

3. The age question

Shortly after you first open the game, it asks you to choose one of three age ranges:

  • 12 or younger;
  • 13 to 17; or
  • 18 or older.

The question is neutral, and every answer can play the game. The selected range is stored on the device and controls which SDK settings the game applies. It is not a verified date of birth or parental-consent process.

  • Before you answer: the game defers its LevelPlay and GameAnalytics initialization. Some Firebase/platform components may initialize at startup; Firebase Analytics collection, advertising-ID collection and Crashlytics collection are configured off by default. We are still validating startup network behavior, so we do not promise that no technical data is processed before the answer.
  • Under 18 (either younger choice): the game applies child-directed ad settings and keeps GameAnalytics and Crashlytics collection off. Ad/mediation providers can still process limited device, network and ad-event data to deliver, secure or measure an ad; details depend on the active providers and settings (see section 9).
  • 18 or older: internal test builds may initialize GameAnalytics and Crashlytics after this answer. The planned public-release flow will ask separately for optional crash reporting and will apply relevant Google consent choices before optional Google analytics or advertising collection. That release flow must be verified before launch.

To answer again, use Settings → Reset progress. This erases local progress and the saved age range, and the Android app closes; reopen it to answer again. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling may also reset local data. Device backup settings can affect restoration.

4. Information we collect

4.1 Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your device. We don't receive this information, and it isn't sent to any of our partners:

  • Progress: Slaps balance, lifetime Slaps, level, high score, games played, power-up levels and charges, unlocked rooms, and timers for ad rewards.
  • Profile: the in-game nickname you choose (or the one the game generates), a random ID that identifies the save file, and gameplay statistics.
  • Settings: music, sound effects, vibration, tutorial progress and your age range.

Your operating system may include this save in your device backup (Android Auto Backup to your Google account, or iCloud/device backup on Apple devices), according to your device settings. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple, not by us.

4.2 Information used for analytics and crash reports

The planned release is intended to limit these optional services to adults and to apply the choices described in section 7. Current internal builds may not yet implement the final consent flow. We will verify the shipped build and update this policy before public release.

  • Gameplay events: test integrations may record runs, level, Slaps, tutorial steps, shop visits, upgrades, power-ups, ad interactions and product views or purchases.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating-system and app versions, language, connection and session diagnostics.
  • Identifiers and network information: SDK-generated installation or analytics identifiers and IP address or similar network information, which providers may use for service operation, approximate region, security and fraud prevention. The exact identifiers depend on the SDK, platform and consent settings.
  • Crash reports: diagnostic details about a crash and limited gameplay context (such as level and lifetime Slaps) if crash reporting is enabled.

4.3 Information involved when ads are shown

Ad mediation and ad networks may receive information such as IP address, device and operating-system details, app version, language, advertising or other identifiers where permitted, and ad request, impression, completion or interaction events. Child-directed settings are applied for under-18 choices, but we will verify each SDK and network's actual data behavior before release. Google's AdMob service is planned but is not active in the current game build.

4.4 Information involved in purchases

When you buy something, the app store processes the payment. The game receives only a confirmation of which product was bought and a transaction reference (see section 8).

4.5 Information you give us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include, such as your device model, screenshots or a store order number. Please don't send us payment card details or other sensitive information.

4.6 What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise (GPS) location, microphone or camera data, health data, or biometric data. We don't run chat, social or user-generated content features.

7. Advertising and tracking

All ads in the game are optional rewarded ads. You choose to watch one in return for a reward, such as a revive or bonus Slaps. You never have to watch an ad to keep playing.

  • Under 18: the current LevelPlay integration applies child-directed and device-ID opt-out settings. Ad providers may still process technical data needed for ad delivery and security. We will verify the actual data sent by every mediation and network SDK before launch; we do not promise that all technical identifiers are absent from every request.
  • Adults: adult ad requests may use identifiers or support personalised advertising only where the user’s choices, platform settings and applicable law permit. Google UMP and AdMob are planned but not yet integrated into the game; until then, the final privacy-choice flow is incomplete and the current test build is not a public-release configuration.
  • Ads are optional rewarded ads when available. Availability, providers, ad content and privacy controls can vary by country and network.

8. In-app purchases

The game sells optional packs of Slaps, an in-game currency. Google Play or the Apple App Store processes every payment, so we never see or store your card, bank, UPI or wallet details. The game receives a confirmation of the product bought and a transaction reference, which it uses to add Slaps to your balance and to prevent duplicates. For adult players, analytics may record which product was bought. Refunds and billing questions are handled under the store's policies; we're glad to help (see Support).

9. Children's privacy

Pesky Pest Fly Quest is intended for a mixed audience, including children. When a player selects either under-18 range, the current game applies child-directed ad settings and disables GameAnalytics and Crashlytics collection. This age-range answer is not verified parental consent. Ad delivery can still involve technical and network data processed by mediation or ad providers.

  • GameAnalytics and Crashlytics collection are kept off for under-18 choices;
  • LevelPlay receives child-directed and device-ID opt-out settings; technical data such as IP address, device/app details and ad events may still be processed by ad partners;
  • we intend not to request personalised or behavioural advertising for under-18 choices, but must verify network-level behavior and child-eligible inventory before launch; and
  • the game asks for no contact details and has no chat, social or sharing features.

The Android test build includes Firebase components that can initialize at app startup, although Firebase Analytics, its advertising-ID collection and Crashlytics collection default to off. We are still verifying pre-age-choice network behavior. The public release must pass that verification before this policy can be treated as final. Parents or guardians can contact us at rockyroy.studios@gmail.com with questions or requests.

If you're a parent or guardian, you can ask us what information relates to your child, ask us to delete it, or ask us to stop it being used, by emailing rockyroy.studios@gmail.com. You can also delete everything on the device by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it. Because the game has no accounts, we may ask for the device model and the approximate dates of play to help find data held by our partners.

For in-app purchases, we recommend using the purchase approval controls in Google Play (Family Link or purchase authentication) or Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

10. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: for ads, purchases, analytics and crash reports.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's Settings.
  • Advertising ID (Android): used for adult players only, as described in section 7.
  • Motion sensors: the game may read how the device is tilted to add a gentle parallax effect to backgrounds. This is processed on the device and never sent anywhere.

The game doesn't request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or notifications.

13. Security

Data sent to our partners travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We limit what we collect to what the game needs, never collect payment details, keep analytics free of names and contact details, restrict access to our partner dashboards to authorised members of our team with strong passwords and two-step verification, and review our partners' security commitments. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects you, we'll notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

14. Your rights and choices

Choices you can make yourself

  • Delete your data on the device: in the game, use Settings → Reset progress, or clear the game's data, or uninstall it.
  • Stop further app collection: uninstalling stops the game from collecting further app data from that device. Data already received by service providers may remain under their retention rules; contact us to ask about deletion.
  • Limit ads: rewarded ads are always optional. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID (Android), or decline or withdraw tracking permission (iPhone/iPad), as described in section 7.

Requests you can send us

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict its use, including for analytics or personalised ads;
  • withdraw consent at any time, which doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew;
  • data portability; and
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapable (India).

Email rockyroy.studios@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. Because the game has no player accounts, details such as device model, operating system and approximate dates of play may help us locate relevant records. We will respond and handle requests within the time and process required by the law that applies to you. We will not ask you to send payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.
